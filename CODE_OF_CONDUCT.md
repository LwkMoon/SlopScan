# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SlopScan's project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) to
be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience
level, background, or how they found the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in disagreements. Rule design and scoring philosophy are
  genuinely debatable -- argue the merits, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most bug reports and "this seems wrong" comments are
  exactly that, not attacks.
- Give and receive feedback constructively, especially on new rules or
  false-positive reports, where the goal is a better detector, not a
  "win."

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Using the project's spaces to promote unrelated products or services.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces -- the repository itself (issues, PRs,
discussions, commits) and any official communication channels.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers
(see repository contact info). Maintainers will review and respond to all
reports, and may take any action they deem appropriate, including warning
the offender, removing content, or banning someone from project spaces.
